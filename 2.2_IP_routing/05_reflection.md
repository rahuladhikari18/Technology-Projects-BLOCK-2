1. Difference between physical cable and IP addressing
  ANSWER :
          1 . Physical Cable
              -Connects devices physically.
              -Carries data between devices.
              -Uses Ethernet cables and connectors.
          2. IP Addressing
              -Gives each device a unique address.
              -Identifies devices on a network.
              -Helps data reach the correct device.
