
# 1. Part A — Crimping Your Ethernet Cable

## 1.1 Steps You Followed
Describe how you:  
- Prepared the cable  
- Arranged the wires (T568B)  
- Inserted & crimped the connector  
ANSWER: First, I prepared the Ethernet cable by cutting it to the required length and carefully stripping off a small section of the outer insulation to expose the four twisted wire pairs. I then separated and straightened the eight wires so they were easier to arrange. Next, I arranged the wires in the correct T568B colour sequence:
- White/Orange
- Orange
- White/Green
- Blue
- White/Blue
- Green
- White/Brown
- Brown
After arranging the wires, I trimmed them evenly to make sure they could fit properly into the RJ45 connector. I carefully inserted all eight wires into the connector, making sure each wire reached the end and stayed in the correct position. Finally, I used a crimping tool to secure the connector firmly to the cable.
